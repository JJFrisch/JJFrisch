## Research

**LEAD Lab, University of Maryland** (Fall 2026 – May 2027, upcoming)  
Undergraduate Research Assistant with Dr. Rachel Romeo (*Language, Experience, and Development* Lab, College of Education). Working on **SLIDE** (Stress and Language Influences on Development in Early childhood): linking moment-to-moment caregiver stress to conversational turns (LENA) and school readiness. Also supporting the **Jacobs CIFAR Research Fellowship** work on neural coupling between teachers and students during classroom learning.

**Quantum Rare Cell Recognition** (2026, ongoing)  
Independent quantum ML project benchmarking PennyLane quantum kernels against classical clustering for rare-cell detection in single-cell RNA-seq data. PennyLane, Qiskit, scikit-learn.

**Anxious Generation Screen-Time Study** (Aug 2024 – Dec 2025)  
Lead researcher. Stratified, cluster-sampled survey of high school students (n ≈ 140). Cleaned heterogeneous iOS/Android usage data; linear regression and t-tests (α = 0.01) found significant associations between weekly phone use, sociability, anxiety, and weekday sleep.

**Zheng Lab, Drexel University** (May – Jul 2025)  
Designed tendon-driven soft robotic manipulators (SpiRob) in SolidWorks and built GPU-accelerated soft-body simulations in NVIDIA Isaac Gym. Fabrication and control work in [SpiRobControls](https://github.com/JJFrisch/SpiRobControls).

**PGSS at Carnegie Mellon University** (Summer 2024)  
Led the BlokusDuo deep RL team. PPO + MCTS self-play with CNN encodings; ~85% win rate over 18,000+ GPU games. Co-authored a peer-reviewed PGSS Journal paper.

**Drexel Bioengineering Summer Program** (Summer 2022)  
Prototyped AR-enhanced physical therapy goggles in Blender, exploring motion tracking and visual feedback for rehab exercises.
